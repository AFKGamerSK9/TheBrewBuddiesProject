A Heritage of High Altitudes

Our Ethiopian Yirgacheffe comes from the family-owned washing station in the Gedeo zone. Grown at altitudes between 1,900 and 2,200 meters, these indigenous heirloom trees produce small, dense beans packed with complex sugars.

Every harvest cherries are hand-sorted, depulped, fermented underwater for 36 hours, and dried slowly on raised wooden beds. This precise process preserves the delicate, tea-like quality that has made Yirgacheffe famous worldwide.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take inside Kathmandu Valley? ▼
Standard delivery inside Kathmandu, Lalitpur, and Bhaktapur takes 24 to 48 hours immediately after our roasting process is completed.
Can I order pre-ground coffee bags? ▼
Yes! Every single origin coffee bag can be custom-ground specifically to match your brewing apparatus (French Press, Drip, Espresso, or Aeropress).
Do you offer international shipping? ▼
Currently, we specialize in delivering freshly preserved roasts within Nepal. Contact our support team for bulk custom wholesale requests abroad.
